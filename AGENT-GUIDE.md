# AGENT-GUIDE — submitting business through the Submission Desk

## ⏰ The 11:00 AM rule — read this first

> **Complete submissions received before 11:00 AM (Trinidad time) get an underwriting response the same day.**
> **Submissions completed after 11:00 AM get a response by 11:00 AM the next business day.**

The clock starts the moment your file is **complete** — every required field filled in, every required document attached — not when you first open it. If you start a file at 10:55 and don't finish it until 11:15, it's treated as an after-cutoff submission. So: gather your documents first, then sit down and do it in one go.

If back office sends a file back marked **Needs Info**, the cutoff clock resets once you resubmit it — same rule applies to the resubmission.

## 1. Signing in

Go to the Submission Desk link, enter the **agent code** and **4-digit PIN** you were given. If you don't have one, ask the back office or admin to add you to the Agents list.

Forgot your PIN? Ask admin — they can see/reset it in two clicks.

---

## 2. Quick Quote — when the client just wants a price, fast

This is what opens first when you sign in, because this is usually where things actually start: a client asking "how much would it cost me" before they've committed to anything. You're often racing another broker on this, so Quick Quote is deliberately tiny — no documents, no forms, just enough to get a number back to the client.

1. Tap **Quick Quote**.
2. Pick the product, enter the client's name and phone, and a note if you need one (e.g. vehicle value, sum insured, whatever you're pricing against).
3. Submit. It shows up in your Quick Quote list with a reference number, and back office can see it too — a quote is still tracked, it's just lighter than a full submission.

**Claims don't go through Quick Quote** — there's nothing to quote on a claim, so that product won't appear in the picker here.

**When the client says yes:** open the quote in your list and tap **"Client is going ahead →."** This carries everything you already entered straight into the full submission wizard, on the **same reference number** — you're not starting a new file, you're continuing the one you've already got. From there you land on step 3 below (open the real form, attach documents) and finish with step 4 (review, submit). The 11:00 AM clock (see above) only starts counting from this point, once the file is actually complete — not from when you first sent the quote.

## 3. Submitting a new file — 4 steps, under 3 minutes on your phone

Tap **New Submission**.

1. **Pick the product.** Motor, Property (Residential/Homesure or Commercial/Fire), Covercare (New or Conversion), Change in Policy, Renewal, Claims (Motor or Property), or Other. Only that product's questions and documents will show up next.
2. **Client basics.** Name, phone number (used for WhatsApp updates), email (optional), and any cover notes.
3. **Fill in the real form + attach documents.** Tap "Open the form" to fill in the actual Tatil form with the client right there — it's the same genAssist form you already know, just saved straight into this submission. Then tick off each required document as you have it, and attach a photo or scan of it. **You can't continue until every box is ticked and at least one file is attached** — this is intentional, it's what stops incomplete files going to the back office.
4. **Review & submit.** Check everything, then tap Submit. You'll get a reference number (e.g. `ARAL-2610-0001`) and the exact date/time back office must get back to you by.

**Drafts autosave.** If you get interrupted partway through, your progress is saved — next time you open New Submission you'll be offered to resume or start fresh.

### Required documents by product

| Product | What you'll need |
|---|---|
| Motor | Valid ID, vehicle registration (certified copy), signed proposal form, photos of all 4 sides of the vehicle, current No-Claims Discount letter (if applicable) |
| Property — Residential (Homesure) | Valid ID, proof of address, signed proposal form, recent photos of the property, list of items & valuations (if All Risks requested) |
| Property — Commercial (Fire) | Valid ID / company registration, signed proposal form, recent photos of the premises, stock/asset valuation (if applicable) |
| Covercare — New | Copy of ID, birth certificate, immunization card (if applicable), utility bill (≤3 months), bank statement, completed KYC, completed proposal form |
| Covercare — Conversion | Copy of ID, birth certificate, immunization card, utility bill (≤3 months), bank statement, completed KYC, medical reports (if requested), premium receipt, stamped deduction order, agent's report, beneficiary ID |
| Change in Policy | Valid ID, signed Change form, supporting documents for the specific change |
| Renewal | Valid ID (if expired), updated answers for anything that's changed, payment confirmation |
| Claims — Motor | Signed Motor Accident Report, police report reference (if applicable), photos of damage, repair estimate, driver's licence copy |
| Claims — Property | Signed Property Claim form, police/fire report reference (if applicable), photos of damage, proof of ownership/receipts |
| Other | Describe what's needed in the notes — back office will confirm |

*Note: the 11:00 AM cutoff applies to everything, including Claims — confirmed, this is the rule for everything sent to CSR in head office.*

## 4. Tracking your files — "My Files"

Every file you've submitted shows up here with a status badge. The intake route is the same for every product:

`Submitted` → `Acknowledged` → (`GM Approval` if that step is switched on) → `Underwriting`

Tap into any file to see the full timeline — every status change and note, with who did it and when.

**After an Approved decision, insurance products (not Claims) also pick up a payment step:** `Approved` → `Payment Pending` → `Payment Received` → `Documents Issued`. Back office will tell you how the client should pay (cash/online at ARAL, or the 2-part option if it's fully comprehensive) once they request payment — you'll see it on the file either way, no separate message needed.

**Once a policy is issued, don't forget the renewal.** About a month before the renewal date, back office spins up a renewal thread linked to the original file (you'll see "Linked to [original reference]" on it) so Tatil can be consulted on claims history, rate changes, and vehicle value before requoting. Treat a renewal thread exactly like a Quick Quote — the same urgency applies once the client confirms they're renewing.

**If a file shows "Needs Info"**: open it, read exactly what back office needs (it's consolidated into one list, not a drip of separate messages), get it from the client, then tap **"I have sent the missing info — notify back office."** That moves it back into the queue and resets the response clock.

**Once underwriting actually responds**, back office records that outcome on your same file (same reference number — never a new submission), and you'll see it two ways on that file's detail screen:
- The status badge updates to one of: `UW Query`, `Approved`, `Declined`, `Documents Issued`, `Closed` — or, for Claims specifically: `Query`, `Approved`, `Repudiated`, `Payment Issued`, `Closed` (claims use different outcome wording since a "decline" there is a repudiation and the payout isn't a policy document).
- A highlighted **"Underwriting feedback"** note appears right under the route stepper with whatever back office recorded — e.g. what underwriting is querying, or the decision and any notes. If your file is sitting at `UW Query`/`Query`, that's your cue to get back office whatever underwriting is asking for so they can resubmit it on the same thread.

Files with no update in 3+ business days get a small warning badge — that's your cue to follow up with back office yourself if you haven't heard anything.

---

## 5. A few things worth knowing

- Nothing in the Submission Desk is sent to Tatil automatically — back office reviews and sends the pack themselves. You're always submitting *to the back office*, not directly to underwriting.
- Your client's phone number is used to build one-tap WhatsApp update links for back office — double-check you've typed it correctly.
- The **Form Library** button in the nav gives you the original genAssist forms directly (for reference, printing, or if you're not submitting anything new) — it's the same tool, just without the wizard wrapped around it.

Questions or stuck? Ask the back office or admin — don't fall back to WhatsApp/email for a live submission, since those files won't show up anywhere in the queue.
