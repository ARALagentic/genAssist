Why is No-Claims Discount split into two questions?
Good catch — "Do you have a No-Claims Discount?" answered "No" could mean two different things: no discount currently, or no claims ever (which is actually good news). Splitting it into a claims-history question and a discount-status question removes the guesswork for underwriting.
Abbreviations on this form
NCD
No-Claims Discount — reduced premium for claim-free years on the last policy.
TPFT
Third Party, Fire & Theft — covers others, plus fire/theft of the proposer's own car.
TPO
Third Party Only — the legal minimum; never covers the proposer's own vehicle.
IDV
Insured Declared Value — the agreed payout ceiling if the car is written off or stolen.
Excess
What the proposer pays first on every claim, before the insurer pays the rest.
CoI
Certificate of Insurance — the legal proof-of-cover document once a policy is active.
TPPD
Third Party Property Damage — damage the proposer's car causes to someone else's property.
OD
Own Damage — the part of a claim covering damage to the proposer's own car.
POA
Power of Attorney — legal permission for someone else to sign on the proposer's behalf.
KYC
Know Your Customer — the identity documents required before cover can start.
U/W
Underwriting / Underwriter — assesses risk and approves the terms of cover.
GM
General Manager — the branch head who countersigns approval in this workflow.
AML
Anti-Money Laundering — the law behind the Due Diligence checklist (Proceeds of Crime Act, 2000).
PEP
Politically Exposed Person — someone (or a close relative/associate) with real political, government, military, or state-corporation power.
FATCA
US Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act — requires reporting US-linked account holders to US tax authorities.
TIEA
Tax Information Exchange Agreement — the treaty basis for sharing account data with other countries' tax authorities.
Indicia
Signs suggesting a client may also be a taxpayer elsewhere — triggers extra FATCA/TIEA paperwork.
W-9
US tax form confirming someone is a US taxpayer, with their US tax ID.
W-8BEN
US tax form confirming someone is foreign, not a US taxpayer.
Payor
Whoever actually pays the premiums — may be a different person from the Policy Holder.
TTD
Trinidad and Tobago Dollar.
Coordination of Benefits
Stops the same medical bill being paid twice when someone holds more than one health policy.
ACH
Automated Clearing House — the electronic transfer system used to pay claim reimbursements into a bank account.
Pre-existing condition
Any illness/injury with advice, treatment, or symptoms before cover started, or starting within 14 days of the policy date. Excluded under CoverCare.
Beneficiary (health policy)
Not a life-insurance death benefit — only receives an already-approved claim left unpaid if the insured dies.
Beneficiary (life policy)
The actual death-benefit recipient — a materially different meaning from "beneficiary" on the health-policy forms.
Trustee
Holds and manages a death benefit on behalf of a beneficiary who can't yet receive it directly, typically a minor.
NIS No.
National Insurance Scheme number — Trinidad & Tobago's social-security-equivalent ID.
Statutory Declaration
A sworn legal affidavit used in place of an original document, typically when a policy has been lost.
Lost Policy Advertisement
A published legal notice announcing a lost policy, required before a replacement or payout can proceed.
Status asthmaticus
A severe asthma attack that doesn't respond to standard treatment — a significant underwriting flag, not a routine attack.
PFT / Spirometry
Pulmonary Function Test — measures lung capacity and airflow (FEV1, FVC, PEF) to assess respiratory conditions.
Rated
A previous insurer accepted an application but at a higher premium for assessed risk — not the same as a decline.
Rider
A condition or exclusion underwriting attaches to a policy, adjusting standard cover for a specific risk.
Co-insurance
The share TATIL pays after the deductible — 80% on CoverCare, leaving the insured the remaining 20%.
Deductible (health)
Paid out of pocket before co-insurance kicks in — $2,500 per disability on CoverCare Major Medical, at every tier.
R+C
Reasonable & Customary charge — on the $300k/$500k Major Medical tiers, surgery pays the going local rate instead of a fixed dollar cap.
Waiting period
Time on cover before a benefit pays out: 30 days for Maternity, 90 days for Vision and Dental.
